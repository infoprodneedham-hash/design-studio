Standard Terms of Craft

1. Complete Digital Sovereignty

The client is always the sole legal owner of their domain name, DNS records, and third-party hosting credentials. All accounts are configured under the client’s designated email address. The practitioner operates solely with temporary or long-term administrative privileges.

2. Transparent Milestone Billing

Initial builds and migration projects are performed against a defined scope sheet and fixed milestone sum. Work begins upon deposit approval, with final assets released upon project sign-off. Change requests outside the agreed milestone are billed via written task quotes prior to commencement.

3. Elastic Ongoing Maintenance

Ongoing maintenance is elastic and strictly non-hostage. The client may schedule as little as 1 hour per quarter or scale up to 10 hours per week for campaign initiatives without long-term binding lock-ins.

4. Open Standards & Portability

Code and design deliverables utilize standard, portable web specifications (HTML5, modern CSS, standards-compliant JS). No obscure proprietary site builders or locked frameworks are enforced.
