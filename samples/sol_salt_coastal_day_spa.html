Our Grounding Ethos

Where the elements of surf, stone, and sun reset your nervous system.

Sol & Salt was conceived as an intentional decompression space along Victoria’s majestic coastline. Positioned just moments from the rugged cliffs of Bells Beach, our sanctuary mirrors the restorative rhythm of maritime waters.

Every facial, massage, and hydrotherapy soak is enriched with pure Torquay sea salt, wild organic coastal tea tree, cold-pressed macadamia oils, and rich Tasmanian kelp bio-actives. We step away from sterile clinical atmospheres to deliver an earthy, sensory sanctuary that reconnects body and soul.
