Our Story & Heritage

An Intimate Coastal Retreat Carved by Tide and Time

Nestled on the bluff above a sheltered tide basin, The Driftwood Cove was originally built in 1928 as a maritime poet's sanctuary. Carefully reimagined into a luxury four-suite bed and breakfast, we preserve the soul of timbered craftsmanship while offering refined modern comforts.

Wander down stone pathways lined with sea lavender and wild cypress directly onto our secluded crescent beach. Whether you are curled up by a stone fireplace listening to crashing waves, or sipping small-batch coastal roast on your deck, our inn provides an unhurried cadence found nowhere else.

Morning by the Sea

Artisanal Mornings & Ocean Mist

Breakfast is never an afterthought at Driftwood Cove. Every sunrise brings a thoughtfully prepared two-course seasonal spread featuring organic produce from coastal ridge micro-farms and eggs gathered just down the road.

Seaside Espresso & Pour-Over Bar

Single-origin beans roasted locally, paired with organic teas and house-pressed lavender oat milk.

Wild Yeast Brioche & Bakes

Warm berry scones, sourdough crumpets, and churned sea-salted Meyer lemon butter made daily.

Farm-to-Table Savory Course
