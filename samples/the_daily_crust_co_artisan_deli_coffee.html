Our Craft & Origins

Rooted in Heritage. Crafted for the Modern Palate.

The 9-Year Mother Starter

Every loaf of sourdough, ciabatta, and seeded rye begins with 'Matilda,' our wild yeast culture nurtured since 2015. We utilize unbleached heritage grains stone-ground in small batches to preserve nutrition, deep aroma, and an open, airy crumb with unmistakable blistered crust.

No Commercial Yeast • Easy Digestion

Old-World Charcuterie

We brine our pastrami with coriander, black peppercorn, and juniper berries for two full weeks before low-and-slow smoking over hickory hardwood. From dry-cured Coppa to crispy rolled Porchetta stuffed with garden herbs, we cut thin slices to melt seamlessly on warm bread.

Pasture-Raised • Ethically Sourced

Third-Wave Coffee Program

Coffee isn't an afterthought; it's our co-star. We roast transparently traded micro-lots in small batches each week. Pulled through a customized Synesso espresso machine with dialed-in mineralized water, producing velvety flat whites and sparkling single-origin pour overs.

Direct Trade • Weekly Fresh Roasts

Why It Tastes Different

Scratch Dressings
