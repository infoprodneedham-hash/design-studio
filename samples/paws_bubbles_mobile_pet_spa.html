Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our driveway grooming visit.

Nope! Our Mercedes Sprinter vans are completely self-sufficient. We carry our own 70-gallon heated fresh water supply, dirty water recovery tank, and whisper-quiet green lithium power systems. We never need your electrical outlets or garden hoses.
To ensure optimal wellness and safety for all our furry clients, we require proof of up-to-date Rabies vaccination for all dogs and cats over 16 weeks of age. DHPP and Bordetella are highly recommended. You can quickly upload your vet paperwork during booking!
We specialize in anxious, timid, and senior pets because our environment is completely quiet and free from barking salon distractions. For pets with known bite tendencies, please let us know beforehand. We use Fear-Free gentle handling techniques and never push a pet past their comfort threshold.
We understand life happens! We kindly ask for at least 48 hours notice to cancel or reschedule without fee so we can reallocate our travel route. Cancellations made under 24 hours are subject to a $45 travel route reservation fee.
We require a relatively flat driveway or legal street parking space directly in front of your home (about 2 normal car lengths or 24 feet). Our vans have high clearance and fit comfortably in standard residential driveways.
