Certified D.O.P. Ingredients

Family Tradition Since 1994

Where Ancient Italian Recipes Meet Modern Passion.

At Bella Notte, we refuse to cut corners. Our pizza dough undergoes a rigorous 72-hour slow cold fermentation using organic unbleached flour, resulting in an airy, leopard-spotted crust that is delightfully crisp and gentle on digestion.

Every morning at 6:00 AM, our pasta artisans extrude bronze-die rigatoni and hand-fold delicate tagliatelle using pasture-raised heritage eggs and Italian semolina. Our sauces simmer for six hours with crushed San Marzano D.O.P. tomatoes, fresh sweet basil, and Ligurian extra-virgin olive oil.
